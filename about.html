About Fry's Excavating & Grading

A local, family-owned excavating contractor committed to quality workmanship and honest service.

Who We Are

Fry's Excavating & Grading is a family-owned and operated excavating contractor based in Godfrey, Illinois. We specialize in providing professional excavating, grading, demolition, hauling, and land clearing services to residential and commercial customers throughout the Metro East area.

Our commitment is simple: deliver quality work at fair prices with honest communication every step of the way. We treat every project, whether big or small, with the same level of care and professionalism.

Our Experience

With years of hands-on experience in the excavating industry, our team has the knowledge and skills to handle a wide range of earthwork projects. From preparing sites for new construction to clearing overgrown land, we've seen it all and know how to get the job done right.

We operate well-maintained equipment and stay current with industry best practices to ensure safe, efficient operations on every job site. Our experience means we can anticipate challenges before they become problems and find practical solutions that keep your project on track.
